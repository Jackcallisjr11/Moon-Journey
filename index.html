A Blessing For Your Journey

"May He grant you your heart's desire and fulfill all your plans."

Psalm 20:4

My Dearest Kat,

Seventeen years live inside these pages — and still, somehow, they hold only a fraction of who you are.

Every year on this list was a year the world was a little brighter because you were in it. Not because you tried to shine, but because you simply did — quietly, faithfully, the way the moon does, night after night, asking nothing in return.

I believe you were formed with a purpose, Kat. Not by accident, not by chance — but on purpose, by a Father who counts your days and calls you His own. "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you." He has walked with you through every one of these years, even the ones that felt dark, even the ones the pictures can't show. Especially those.

So today, I don't just wish you a happy birthday. I bless you. I bless the year ahead of you — may it be soft where you need softness, strong where you need strength, and full of the quiet, steady light you've always given everyone else.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for letting me walk a little of this journey beside you.
